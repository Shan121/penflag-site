Penflag privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · applies to Penflag 0.2.0 and later

Short version: your notes stay on your device. Penflag has no servers and sends nothing anywhere.

What Penflag does

Penflag is a browser extension for private notes and tags about the LinkedIn people, companies, jobs and posts you care about. You write them in the browser's side panel. Penflag never clicks, posts or acts on LinkedIn for you, and it doesn't change LinkedIn's pages.

Information we collect

None. Penflag has no servers, analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts, and it makes no network requests. We, the developers, never see your notes or anything else about you.

Information stored on your device

  • Your notes. For each LinkedIn page you write about: the page's address, a title, your note, your tags, and when you created and last changed it. They are kept in the extension's own storage in this browser profile (IndexedDB). They are not synced to other devices.
  • One setting: whether the experimental Feed Freeze add-on is on (it is off by default).

Everything is removed when you uninstall Penflag, or when you use “Delete all” on its settings page.

What Penflag reads on LinkedIn

On www.linkedin.com, Penflag reads only the page's address and title, so the side panel knows which person, company, job or post you are looking at, and so the toolbar icon can show a dot on pages that have a note. It doesn't read page content, messages, connections or anything else.

If you turn on the Feed Freeze add-on, it also stops the LinkedIn feed page from noticing that you left the tab, so the feed doesn't reload. It reads no content either.

Export and import

“Export notes” saves a file with all your notes to your computer. That file is yours: Penflag doesn't send it anywhere, so keep it as safe as you would keep the notes themselves. “Import” reads a file you choose and adds its notes to Penflag on this device.

Permissions

  • Storage, to keep the Feed Freeze setting.
  • Side panel, to show your notes next to the page.
  • Running on www.linkedin.com, to read the page address and title as described above (and, only if you turn it on, for Feed Freeze).

Sharing

Penflag collects no data, so there is nothing to sell, share or transfer.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Any change that adds data collection would be announced in the extension's release notes before it takes effect.
